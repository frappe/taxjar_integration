Scheduled Jobs ​

The app runs these jobs on a schedule. You do not start them.

How OftenJobWhat It DoesRuns When Off
Every 15 MinutesInvoice RetrySends failed invoices and cancellations again, when the error is temporary.-
Customer RetrySends failed customers again, when the error is temporary. A customer that waited more than 15 minutes in the queue becomes Failed first.-
DailyNexus UpdateGets the nexus states of each company from TaxJar. On a failure, it sends a notification to the TaxJar users. Refer to Daily Update Failure.-
Log CleanupDeletes the TaxJar API Log entries that are older than Retention. With 0, it keeps all entries.●
Usage SummarySends a summary of the use of the app, only when usage data is on. Refer to Usage Data & Feedback.●
WeeklyCategory UpdateGets the TaxJar product tax categories, and adds only the new ones.-

Runs When Off: the job also runs when TaxJar is off in TaxJar Settings. The other jobs do nothing then.

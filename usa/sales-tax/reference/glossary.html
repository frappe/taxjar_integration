Glossary

Glossary ​

Sales Tax ​

TermMeaning
Destination-based taxThe state uses the rate of the ship-to address.
ExemptionA customer or a sale that pays no sales tax, for example a reseller. Refer to Customer Tax Exemption.
Exempt regionA US state or Canadian province where a customer's exemption applies.
ExportA sale with a ship-to country that is not the United States. It has no US sales tax.
JurisdictionA level of government that charges sales tax: the state, the county, the city or a special district.
NexusA legal connection to a state. You collect sales tax only in your nexus states. Refer to Updating your nexus.
Origin-based taxThe state uses the rate of the ship-from address.
Product tax categoryA TaxJar code that tells TaxJar how states tax an item. Refer to Item Tax Exemption.

Documents & Sync ​

TermMeaning
Credit noteA sales invoice with Is Return (Credit Note) set. TaxJar records it as a refund. Refer to Credit & Debit Notes.
Debit noteA sales invoice that adds an amount to an earlier invoice. TaxJar records it as an order.
Ship fromThe company address with Preferred Billing Address set.
Ship toThe shipping address of the document. If it is empty, the app asks for one when you save.
SyncThe app sends a submitted invoice, credit note or customer to TaxJar. Refer to Syncing to TaxJar.
TaxJar Sales Tax templateThe tax template that TaxJar Setup makes for each company, with a shipping row and a sales tax row.
TransactionAn order or a refund in TaxJar, made from an invoice or a credit note.

TaxJar Account ​

TermMeaning
API tokenThe key from TaxJar that lets the app use one TaxJar account. Each company has a Live token and a Sandbox token.
AutoFileA TaxJar service that files your sales tax returns from the transactions in your TaxJar account.
Live modeThe API mode for real business. TaxJar records the transactions. Refer to Before you go live.
PersonaA type of TaxJar user: TaxJar Manager or TaxJar User. It is not an ERPNext role. Refer to Roles & Permissions.
Sandbox modeThe API mode for tests. TaxJar does not record the transactions.
